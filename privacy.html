IVY Privacy Policy

IVY is a personal AI assistant application.

Information Access

IVY may access Google Gmail and Google Calendar data when the user explicitly authorizes the application.

Use of Information

Gmail and Calendar information is used only to provide features requested by the user, such as email and calendar briefings.

Data Sharing

IVY does not sell or publicly share the user's Gmail or Calendar information.

Data Storage

IVY only processes information required to provide its requested functionality.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the account associated with the IVY project.
